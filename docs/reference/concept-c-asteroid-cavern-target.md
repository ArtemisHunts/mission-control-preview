## Correction — 2026-04-29
The active target is **not** the later oval/portal-style cavern reference we drifted toward. The correct target is a cinematic **exterior cutaway of a single asteroid base** floating in starfield.

This changes the optimization target:
- Less “looking through a perfect dark oval portal.”
- More “full asteroid body with the front carved open so we see the base inside.”

## Non-negotiable target read
The scene must read like:

1. **One complete asteroid body in space**, with the outer silhouette visible against the starfield.
2. **A large irregular front cutaway/cavity**, not a clean circular/oval aperture overlay.
3. **Thick rocky shell and chipped rim** around the opening: top cap, left wall, right cheek, lower foreground lip.
4. **Grey rocky exterior with authored crags**, especially bright top/right rim planes and darker underside mass.
5. **Industrial base embedded inside the hollow**, sitting on layered decks carved into the asteroid.
6. **Central circular lower pit/shaft** near the lower middle/front, with ring levels and small blue/cyan lights.
7. **Large rear/upper hangar or tunnel opening** slightly right of center with cold exterior light pouring in.
8. **Cranes, gantries, platforms, ramps, vehicles/containers, and tiny human-scale lights** distributed across the interior.
9. **Warm amber work lights and small cyan utility lights**, not a giant flat blue portal.
10. **Readable scale at thumbnail**: asteroid mass first, cutaway facility second, micro-detail third.

## What to stop doing
- Stop building or preserving a perfect RingGeometry oval; that was optimizing toward the wrong target.
- Stop treating the shot as a foreground portal/window looking into a cavern.
- Stop making the right opening the dominant hero shape; in the actual reference it is a rear hangar/tunnel inside the asteroid, not the whole composition.
- Stop burying the asteroid exterior silhouette in black masks. The full asteroid body must be visible.
- Stop adding tiny lights before the asteroid body and carved shell read correctly.
