# Concept C asteroid cavern target reference

Source: Michael supplied Discord reference image on 2026-04-28 after HIFI-06.
Image: `docs/reference/concept-c-asteroid-cavern-target.png`

## Correction
The current Concept C hi-fi scene does **not** match this target. It has been optimizing a procedural cutaway/diorama shape. The target is a cinematic hollow asteroid cavern.

## Non-negotiable target read
The scene must read like:

1. **One enormous hollow asteroid body**, not scattered panels around a room.
2. **Dark oval foreground aperture** framing the entire composition, with thick rock silhouette at top/bottom/side edges.
3. **Starfield visible outside and around the asteroid**, especially through the outer contour and the large right-side opening.
4. **Interior industrial city/facility embedded inside the hollow**, not placed in front of rock.
5. **Huge right-side hangar/window opening to space** with bright cold exterior light and visible asteroid/space beyond.
6. **Blue/cyan cold left bay** and **warm amber industrial core** with small practical lights and atmospheric haze.
7. **Large central circular pit/shaft** with believable scale, depth, ring levels, and blue glow.
8. **Layered decks, cranes/gantries, bridges, tiny scale lights/vehicles/workers** to sell a massive inhabited facility.
9. **Rock material hierarchy**: broad sculpted dark crust, mid-scale ridges/strata, chipped rims, crater scars, small detail last.
10. **Cinematic depth**: foreground black rock, mid facility, rear cavern, bright exterior openings.

## What to stop doing
- Stop treating local asteroid texture/detail improvements as success if the overall silhouette/composition is wrong.
- Stop adding isolated procedural panels and shards that read like a toy cutaway.
- Stop grading up because vertex count increased.
- Stop letting the interior layout remain a flat horizontal toy city; it needs cavern-scale depth.
